# Deni

**Know who owes you, and get paid.**

Deni is a simple credit ledger for small shops, kiosks, salons, mechanics and anyone who sells on credit. Write down what each customer takes, record what they pay back, and see at a glance who owes you and how much.

**Open the app:** https://drama-king.github.io/Deni/

## What it does

- Keeps a running balance for every customer
- Records credit given and payments received, each with a short note such as "sugar, bread"
- Shows the total owed to you at the top of the screen
- Puts the customers who owe the most first, and lets you search by name
- Sends a ready-written payment reminder to a customer on WhatsApp
- Copies a list of everyone who owes you, so you can paste it anywhere
- Works in English and Kiswahili, with a language switch at the top right
- Lets you set your own currency and country dial code
- Works without internet once it has been opened for the first time

## How to use it

1. Type a customer's name (and phone number, if you want to send reminders) and tap **Add customer**.
2. Tap the customer to open their page.
3. Enter an amount and an optional note, then tap **Add credit** when they take something, or **Record payment** when they pay.
4. When a customer has a balance and a phone number, tap **Remind on WhatsApp** to send a friendly reminder.

A customer who has paid in full shows **Settled**. A customer who has paid more than they owed shows as being in credit.

## Put it on your phone

- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, open the browser menu, and tap **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Deni then opens full screen from its own icon, like any other app.

## Your data stays with you

Everything you enter is saved on your own phone. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is shared with anyone. The same goes the other way: your records won't appear on a different phone, and clearing your browser data will erase them.

## Languages

English and Kiswahili. Deni picks one from your phone's language and remembers your choice.
